# Mattress count at closeout — Verge Valet bulk crews

> One-page crew brief. Print version generated from this file in D&M document style.
> Live from **01/08/2026**. Applies to **Verge Valet BULK (general) stops only** —
> green-pass crews, Kwinana crews and rangers: nothing changes for you.

## What's new

WMRC needs to know how many mattresses we collect. Mattresses ride the bulk pile
with no booking line of their own, so from now on **the bulk crew counts them at
every stop closeout**. The app makes this required — you can't close a bulk stop
without answering.

## What you'll see

- On the **run sheet**, bulk stops now show **Enter Count** instead of the quick
  **Done ✓** button.
- On the **stop screen**, there's a new **Mattresses Collected** counter above the
  close-out buttons. It starts at **0**.

## The three steps

1. **Open the stop** — tap the stop card or its **Enter Count** button.
2. **Set the count** — tap **+** for every mattress or bed base you put on the
   truck at that stop. Nothing there? Leave it at **0** — zero is a real answer
   and it's one tap.
3. **Close out as normal** — Complete, NCN or Nothing Presented. The count is
   required on **all three** paths (for Nothing Presented it's almost always 0).

## Rules that matter

- **Count what actually went on the truck at that stop.** Each mattress or bed
  base = 1. Don't estimate across the street or the day — per stop.
- **0 is the answer at most stops.** Don't skip it, don't guess it up.
- **Check the number before you tap.** Once the stop is closed the count is
  locked — you can't edit it afterwards.
- **Got it wrong?** Tell your supervisor the same day. The office can correct it,
  but only if they know.
- **Switching between Complete and NCN forms keeps your count** — you won't lose
  it moving around the screens.

## Why it matters

This number goes straight onto the council's reports. WMRC reads it. An honest
count — including honest zeros — is what keeps the reporting solid.

**Questions:** ask your supervisor, or the office.
